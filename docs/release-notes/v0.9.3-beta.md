# AxisWright v0.9.3-beta

**AxisWright** is a free, open-source parametric 3D CAD application for Android tablets, smartphones, and Chromebooks. It combines exact B-rep geometry powered by Open CASCADE Technology with touch, stylus, and mouse controls, plus an optional OpenSCAD-compatible script editor powered by Manifold. The app works 100% offline with no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet permission.

## Highlights

- **Open Source & Free for Everyone:** Now licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later**. All Pro paywalls, Google Play Billing, and the `INTERNET` permission have been removed—STEP export, 3MF export, and the parameter table are available to everyone.
- **Renamed to AxisWright:** Rebranded from ParamWorker with new icons, splash screen, and `.axiswright.json` project format.
- **Bilingual UI:** Complete English and German localization across the app and core geometry/solver messages.
- **2D Sketching:** Added **Transform** (move, rotate, scale, copy selection) and **Text** (closed sketch outlines for engraving/embossing).
- **3D Modeling:** Added **Extrude** modes (*Intersect*, *Two Sides* with independent lengths, *Up to Face*), **Holes** with counterbore, countersink, and metric standards (**M3–M10**), **3D Section View** with dynamic cutting plane, and **formula fields** for patterns, work planes, and hole depths.
- **Stability & Performance:** Resolved native JNI/B-rep lifecycle crashes and deadlocks, eliminated UI hangs during sketching/3D picking/saving on exit, and improved solver and thin-ring region stability.

For the full list of changes, see [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Installation

Download the `foss` APK matching your device architecture from the [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/KevBo8327/AxisWright/releases) page:

- **`AxisWright-0.9.3-beta-foss-arm64-v8a.apk`** — for most Android smartphones and tablets (ARM64).
- **`AxisWright-0.9.3-beta-foss-x86_64.apk`** — for Android emulators and Intel/AMD Chromebooks.

Open the downloaded `.apk` on your device and allow installation from your browser or file manager if prompted.

## Migrating from ParamWorker

AxisWright uses a new Android application ID (`io.github.kevbo8327.axiswright`), so it **installs side-by-side** alongside older `ParamWorker` builds without overwriting their data.

To transfer your existing projects:
1. Open the old ParamWorker app and export your models/scripts as project files.
2. Open **AxisWright** and import those files via **Project menu → Import**. Legacy `.paramworker.json`, `.partwright.json`, `androidcad-model`, and `.scad` files remain fully readable.

## Known Limitations

- **Beta status:** AxisWright is under active development; please double-check critical dimensions before manufacturing or 3D printing.
- **Donations:** Voluntary donation links in the `foss` variant will follow in a later release (the support menu entry is hidden until real donation URLs are configured).
